Why bother putting chores on a blockchain?

Housework Credits is a real, working app where a family — Mom, Dad, and Kid — send each other credits for chores done, with every transfer recorded permanently. It's a small, everyday example that makes the actual benefits of blockchain click: no one can quietly edit the numbers, no one device can lose the whole history, and anyone can check the record for themselves. This quiz walks through why that matters, using this app as the running example — zero coding knowledge required.
